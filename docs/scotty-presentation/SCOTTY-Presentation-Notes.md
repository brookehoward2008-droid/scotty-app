# SCOTTY presentation talking points

Opening: SCOTTY connects daily commuters through private ships and shared rocket buses. The passenger is Atreyu.

1. Plan: Pick a route and departure window. Compare regular, express and local services without leaving the map.
2. Compare: Choose an on-demand private ship or a shared rocket bus. The wedge-shaped Star Destroyer buses have 80 or 120 seats, with available seats and per-seat fares shown alongside the pilot.
3. Trust: Open a pilot's rating and passenger reviews before deciding. The review panel leads directly to pilot selection.
4. Review: Confirm the destination, pilot, departure and total in a dedicated review step.
5. Book: Review a saved method and give a clear confirmation. The classroom build does not process payments.
6. Depart: See the selected craft and pilot, then follow the vessel on the route.
7. Seattle: Connect the space experience to a familiar street map with fictional launch terminals and invented nearby travelers.
8. Solar System: Explore the astronomical chart with time-based planetary positions.

Closing: Beam me up, Scotty. Your ship. Your rocket bus. Your daily ride. The design uses one continuous map environment and progressive booking panels to keep spatial context visible throughout the journey.

Presentation production: Screen recording of the working app, 1440 x 1000 pixels, silent WebM with embedded explanatory captions. Existing app art and model assets were reused; no new artwork was generated. Fictional transport and review data are class assignment fixtures.
